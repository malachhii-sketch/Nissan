About this data

Customers come from Nissan's Open Recall Manifest for Nissan of Macomb. Only people on the team list can sign in and see them. Statuses, appointments and the contact log are shared with the whole team.

The manifest's notice says Do Not Call, autodialer and CAN-SPAM rules can apply to these phone numbers and emails. Contact customers one at a time from your own phone or email, follow your dealership's policy, and mark anyone who asks to stop as Do not contact.

Manifest campaign codes (like R22C9) are Nissan's. The Recalls tab uses NHTSA's numbers (like 23V093000) and covers 2016–2026 models, pulled from NHTSA's database on Oct 14, 2026. A recall listed for a model and year covers some or all of those vehicles; only a VIN check shows whether a specific car is included and still open.

A dashed year in the Recalls tab means NHTSA's database didn't return a list for that exact model and year. Fix type groups each NHTSA remedy's wording to help plan van jobs; your service manager makes the call on what the van can do.
